About

By day I do device validation at Flock, which mostly means finding the ways things break before customers do. Before that: GPU image processing at EM Photonics and radar software at Lockheed Martin.

At Georgia Tech I ran the Solar Racing team — a 16-person crew, a 5,000-mile race across the country, and a better education in shipping hardware on a deadline than any class gave me.

These days my projects start wherever life is mildly annoying me:
